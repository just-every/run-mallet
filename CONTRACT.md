# Mallet product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Mallet is a complete shop management system for US independent auto repair shops replacing Tekmetric, Shopmonkey, Mitchell 1 Manager SE, Shop-Ware or AutoLeap. It runs vehicle intake, digital inspection, exact customer authorization, parts sourcing and stock, technician work, payment, return and continuing service follow-up. Its asset model also serves bicycle, electronics and equipment benches with their appropriate suppliers and diagnostics.

This is the product being built towards, not a claim that today’s implementation already delivers it. The final status table distinguishes current code from the destination. Each clause states buyer behavior and acceptance through a public screen, form, command/API, download or provider event. Provider tests use authorized sandboxes or controlled network edges; they do not prove production eligibility, actual money movement, OEM authorization or physical safety. A missing adapter, provider agreement, native app or financing program is required engineering work, never a scope exclusion.

The complete job includes commercial/fleet customers, vehicle and asset history, appointment and bay capacity, DVI evidence, line-level approvals and supplemental work, automotive labor guides and parts networks, inventory/cores/returns, technician clocks, quality/warranty, customer communication, card/ACH/financing, accounting and ARO/car-count/profit reporting. Named migration paths include every approved auto incumbent and the researched bench-system alternatives. Imported history remains useful in daily repair, follow-up and reporting rather than an inaccessible attachment dump.

Neighboring products stay separate: [Pier](https://github.com/just-every/run-pier) is an optional inventory authority; [Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry), QuickBooks Online or Xero hold the general ledger; [Crank](https://github.com/just-every/run-crank) runs cross-product automation; [Auth](https://github.com/just-every/run-auth) can supply identity. Repair stock, consent, technician work and shop customer follow-up remain Mallet’s job. A connected storefront, accounting package, supplier or OEM does not become part of Mallet.

## Operating rules

- US-first defaults are USD, US contact/address formats, miles with explicit alternative units, jurisdiction-specific parts/labor tax and each shop’s US timezone/DST. Existing records migrate to the US product’s USD labels without changing recorded cent amounts or their frozen tax basis. An unknown shop sales tax rate must be entered before new estimates.
- Estimates, consent, orders and money name exact inputs and versions. A price, part, scope or recipient change invalidates the affected approval. Accepted work is never retrospectively broadened.
- Customer viewing, message delivery, order proposal, bank screenshot and a model’s inference are not approval, supplier acknowledgment, settled payment or physical receipt. Their actual human/provider evidence is retained separately.
- Every scheduled/agent action has a visible policy, owner, outcome and recovery path. Replays and restarts cannot double-charge, double-order or send obsolete approvals. Draft-only authority remains draft-only.
- Every server door enforces current role and installation/location/customer boundaries. Secrets stay private; product records, source evidence, audit and exportable history stay under the shop owner’s custody.
- Existing clause IDs are retained. Their narrow exact-decision, immutable-export and custody guarantees underpin the target; register-only migration and read-only catalog access describe current gaps, not limits on the destination.

## Published workshop guarantees

- **HTTP-001 — Private app boundary.** Hono owns health and product APIs; native owner/operator sessions protect workshop pages, commands and downloads. Every transport checks role, exact configured host and same-origin mutations. Unknown paths return real 404s. Agent bearer access has only AGENT-001 preparation authority. **Acceptance evidence:** Request private pages, state, commands and exports signed out, as another role, after revocation and cross-origin; require refusal without writes, then succeed with the authorized session.

- **WORKSHOP-001 — Register and visits.** Customer-linked assets have unique case-insensitive identifiers and retain every native visit. Booking snapshots customer and asset details, normalizes timezone-bearing ISO timestamps to UTC, assigns the operator ID, and refuses overlaps for either the bay or technician. **Acceptance evidence:** Register an asset through the counter form, book repeat visits and try overlapping bay and technician bookings through the command API; inspect the conflict and complete service history.

- **ESTIMATE-001 — Exact decision.** Estimates retain revisions, prices and preparer identity. A person approves the exact current revision before a recorded manual handoff. Staff capture customer identity, selected lines, method and evidence against it; manual sharing is labeled separately from delivered messaging. Accepted scope is immutable; EST-004 adds separately authorized supplements to that repair. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare and revise through the API, refuse stale approval, record a printed handoff and partial customer authorization, then refuse unapproved quantities and changes to accepted lines.

- **WORK-001 — Authorized actuals.** Parts and labor actuals use only lines and quantities within the person-approved, customer-authorized revision. A member records work only on assigned jobs; completion requires recorded work. Additional scope never inherits consent from a previous decision. **Acceptance evidence:** As an assigned member record an authorized line; refuse another job, an unapproved line and excess quantity, then complete and inspect actor and revision evidence.

- **INVOICE-001 — One saved export.** An invoice freezes authorized prices and actual quantities after completion and requires a person to approve before export. Repeated export retrieves the same saved invoice and journal entry. Saved exports use USD unit, subtotal, sales tax and total columns. Migration preserves original cent amounts, export identities and approvals while updating legacy labels; subsequent downloads return the same saved export. **Acceptance evidence:** Complete authorized work, prepare and approve the invoice through the app, export twice with current and preceding versions, and compare saved identity, lines, totals and approval history.

- **AGENT-001 — Prepare, then person decides.** Expiring revocable bearer keys read state and invoke only estimate.prepare, estimate.revise, dispatch.prepare and invoice.prepare. They cannot approve, share, authorize, export, erase or manage access. Preparation and decisions retain actor, timestamp and exact revision; authority is rechecked at write time. Broader service-advisor actions require separate policy-scoped permissions under ADV-008. **Acceptance evidence:** Issue a preparation key through the owner door, prepare a draft, refuse each decision command, approve as a person and revoke the key; old key and concurrent revoked authority cannot write.

- **IMPORT-001 — Reviewed register move.** Saved RepairShopr customer and asset JSON snapshots use the Base import kit. Preview writes no business records; apply requires the reviewed fingerprint, retains originals, source links and a journal, and supports bounded continuation. Undo refuses changed or used records. MOVE-009 extends the existing register move to repair history. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload customers and customer-assets API snapshots, preview and apply the fingerprint, replay, resume and undo; inspect originals and journal, and prove an asset used by a new visit survives undo.

- **DATA-001 — Custody and erasure.** Native Base portability includes workshop records, exact decisions, saved exports and import custody. Restore needs an empty destination and fresh owner/configuration, excluding identities and credentials. Owner-only ERASE deletes unused assets; assets with visits remain. An archive older than erasure is refused when the destination knows the later erasure or the later journal is supplied; a post-erasure archive restores. A fresh destination given no later evidence can restore the older archive, labelled “Erasure history unverified: supply your latest archive as the journal.” The restore screen asks for the latest journal before accepting, or the owner explicitly chooses unverified recovery. Restoration never silently filters erased rows or claims freshness without that evidence. **Acceptance evidence:** Export populated records through portability routes, restore to an empty fresh installation and compare history; reject populated restore, unauthorized erase and pre-erasure archives; restore a post-erasure backup.

- **PIER-001 — Read only.** The optional Pier catalog adapter performs GET using private authorized connection settings, identifies observation time and reports failures. Catalog browsing itself never moves stock. PART-003 defines a separate explicit reservation/movement connection when Pier is selected as inventory authority; Mallet still owns the repair. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect a real Pier installation, open the catalog and compare SKU/name/unit with source; revoke access and show a recoverable error while source stock remains unchanged.

## Target clauses

### Customers, vehicles and intake

- **CUS-001 — Customer and fleet accounts.** Maintain searchable people and business accounts, multiple vehicles/assets, phone/email, billing contacts, communication preferences, tax status, notes and duplicate-safe merge with source history; fleets can have separate driver, authorizer and payer. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a household and fleet, merge a duplicate, change a driver and search by name, phone and plate; old invoices retain their snapshots and the payer alone sees fleet billing.

- **VEH-001 — Vehicle identity and VIN decode.** Store VIN, plate/state, year/make/model, engine, trim, drivetrain, fuel and vehicle-specific fitment; scan VIN/plate from a phone and decode through NHTSA vPIC and a licensed US plate provider such as CarMD. A staff correction keeps raw decode provenance and never conflates two vehicles. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Scan a VIN and state plate through intake, compare decoded engine/trim with provider evidence, correct an ambiguous result and show duplicate warnings and manual entry when decode is unavailable.

- **VEH-002 — Mileage and service intervals.** Record intake/out mileage or hours, units, observation date and corrections with reason; retain readings per visit, flag impossible regressions and compute time/mileage service due without inventing a current odometer. Readings are nonnegative whole miles (default), kilometers or hours, with timezone-bearing observations stored in UTC; corrections append with a reason and retain the original. A service becomes due when its latest observed reading in the same unit reaches or exceeds the entered threshold, or its inclusive UTC due date arrives; the two triggers use OR and no current reading is extrapolated. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Enter 62,100 miles, close at 62,112, record a corrected prior reading and inspect due dates and the warning for a lower uncorrected reading.

- **VEH-003 — CARFAX service history exchange.** With the shop’s eligible CARFAX account, retrieve vehicle service history and submit eligible completed service records; preserve provider attribution, consent, event IDs and submission failures. External history remains distinguished from shop-performed work. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect a CARFAX approved test account, read history by VIN, close a qualifying repair and submit once; duplicate events do not duplicate history and a provider refusal remains actionable.

- **INTAKE-001 — Condition media and custody.** Attach timestamped before/after photos and video, damage marks, accessories, keys, valuables, security-lock handling and custody receipts to the asset and visit; private secrets are restricted and removed after their purpose ends. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Check in a vehicle with body damage and spare key and a device with charger, capture signed custody receipt and photos, then compare the actual handover against the same checklist.

- **INTAKE-002 — Custom forms and signed shop terms.** Configure forms by vehicle/device type, capture symptom, diagnostic fee, storage/teardown terms and customer consent with exact version, signature and delivery copy; flag missing required fields without losing a draft. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Publish two intake templates, change shop terms after an intake and retrieve its original signed version; a customer completes required fields by phone and receives a copy.

- **INTAKE-003 — Counter and after-hours check-in.** Provide walk-in, drop-box and customer mobile check-in with requested work, contact/vehicle verification, key drop and queue receipt; an advisor confirms booking and diagnostic spend before work starts. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit an after-hours check-in, review it at the counter, match an existing vehicle and authorize the diagnostic fee; duplicate submission creates one intake and no automatic repair authorization.

- **BOOK-001 — Online appointment booking.** Customers book, reschedule or cancel services against published opening hours, bay skills, duration, location, lead time and staff capacity; simultaneous last-slot requests cannot overbook and tentative slots have expiry. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Embed a booking form, choose a service on a phone and contend for the last available bay; one confirmed appointment is sent and cancellation frees capacity.

- **BOOK-002 — Resource calendars and waitlist.** Advisors see day/week technician, bay, lift and loaner capacity, absences and recurring bookings; dispatch approval rechecks conflicts, and a cancellation offers a waitlisted customer a bounded reservation. US shop timezones and DST are explicit. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Book a bay and technician, attempt an overlap, approve a move after a competing booking and exercise a DST transition and waitlist cancellation offer.

- **DIAG-001 — Diagnostic findings and private notes.** Technicians retain complaint/cause/correction, measurements, DTCs, test method and attachments with staff-only notes visibly separated from the customer explanation; no private note leaks through an approval link. Diagnostic entries record a required complaint, cause, correction, exact uppercase OBD-II codes (such as P0301), measurements with their exact value and unit and no conversion, test method, server-verified references to completed DVI-002 media on the same repair, a staff-only note and a customer explanation; the assigned technician or owner/admin records them, and each change appends a revision. Only owner/admin share one exact revision with the customer; a later revision stays private until shared. Owner/admin create read-only customer inspection links: 256 random bits shown once, only the SHA-256 stored outside archives (restores keep link history, never access), 1–30 day expiry (default 7), revocable, GET-only with no session; they show the vehicle, repair title and shared revisions’ complaint, cause, correction, codes, measurements, test method and customer explanation, never staff notes, attachments, prices, costs, contacts or other repairs. Unknown, expired and revoked links get the same not-available page; each view appends a receipt and changes no approval. Mallet does not send the link and labels it not sent; DVI-003 adds shared media, delivery and view analytics and MSG-001 adds sending. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Enter DTCs and measured findings on an assigned repair, write a private cost note, and open the customer inspection link; only explicitly shared findings appear.

- **ASSET-001 — Bicycle and device repair benches.** The same customer/asset/visit model supports bicycle serials, frame and component specifications, equipment hours and device serial/IMEI, model, accessories and bench resources; vehicle-only fields and automotive provider requirements do not block those repairs. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Run bicycle brake work and a serialized device repair from intake to paid return beside an auto repair; their history, parts and tax rules use the correct asset type.

### Digital inspections and repair quality

- **DVI-001 — Configurable digital inspections.** Create versioned multipoint templates by vehicle/service, assign technicians and mark green/yellow/red or not inspected with measurements and mandatory reasons; incomplete checks remain visible and templates can map findings to canned jobs. Owner/admin manage reusable templates and versioned canned service recipes; each assignment snapshots its exact template and mapped recipe revisions for the repair’s current native technician. Only the currently assigned technician records immutable observations; owners/admin read shop inspections, technicians read only their currently assigned repair snapshots, and agents have no inspection-record, event or raw-source access. Every result needs evidence/reason. Yellow/red results also need a configured exact-unit numeric measurement or owner-defined labelled grade, and a completed same-repair private DVI-002 image; the write binds its native custody, digest and provenance. A not-inspected point stays incomplete without invented values. New observations append and supersede earlier results without changing them. Template/recipe edits never alter earlier assignments, results or prepared estimates. A mapped yellow/red finding prepares a real part/labor estimate at the explicitly reviewed current recipe revision and shop tax, preserving original finding, template, mapped recipe and priced recipe snapshots; approval and work still need separate exact decisions. Existing decided repairs use EST-004 supplements. Checklist measurements, grade labels, retained observations and their original photos also appear beside prior/current visits in DVI-004 history, under the existing native assignment check for each visit. All reusable/assigned history, grade labels, photo references and bytes inherit OPS-001 portability with fresh native authority. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Assign a brake inspection, record pad thickness and an uninspected item, change the template and reopen the original; the original checklist and its completion state remain exact.

- **DVI-002 — Photo and video inspection evidence.** Capture, annotate and upload findings on phones/tablets with compression, resumable progress, capture time and technician provenance; keep originals and shared versions and recover interruption without duplicate findings. Owner/admin sessions may access all shop findings; technicians access only their currently assigned repairs, and agents have no media access. Every upload, resume and download rechecks native authority and assignment; writes are same-origin. Findings and uploads bind opaque client IDs and exact metadata, 64 KiB chunk ordinals and SHA-256; mismatched retries refuse, and completion verifies the whole digest before viewing. JPEG/PNG/WebP originals are at most 8 MiB and MP4/WebM originals at most 32 MiB. The phone camera/file door retains original bytes; the annotated shared photo is a JPEG at quality 0.78 with its longest edge at most 1600 pixels. Browser video capture records up to 30 seconds at requested video/audio bitrates of 1,500,000/64,000 bits per second; a prepared shared video retains the captured compressed bytes. Capture time is technician-reported with a timezone and normalized to UTC, separate from server receipt time and native actor provenance. Both versions stay private, with no public URLs; “shared” means prepared, not sent. The browser remembers only opaque finding/upload IDs and content hashes for reselect-and-resume. DVI-003 owns reviewed customer delivery and must preserve private-original control; GOV-006 owns retention, erasure and holds, with no automatic media deletion before that policy ships. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Capture marked-up brake photos and a short video, interrupt the upload, resume, and view original/shared media from the finding; duplicates and private originals are controlled.

- **DVI-003 — Customer inspection presentation.** Text/email a branded mobile report showing measurements, urgency, technician evidence and an advisor-reviewed plain-language explanation, linked to exact estimate lines; record delivery and views without treating a view as approval. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. Customer delivery must preserve DVI-002 private-original control; a prepared shared variant is never itself delivery. It extends the DIAG-001 customer inspection link (token, expiry, revocation, view receipts and the staff-note boundary) with shared media, delivery and view analytics. **Acceptance evidence:** Send an inspection report to a customer phone, play its video and open a proposed repair; a viewed report leaves all approval decisions unchanged.

- **DVI-004 — Inspection history comparison.** Compare prior inspection measurements and images for the same vehicle/asset, distinguish imported evidence, and carry unresolved recommendations forward without silently representing old observations as new tests. Measurements retain the entered numeric value, name and explicit unit; comparison never converts units. Imported attribution is source-declared, not provider-verified. A carry is a reference to the original observation, date, actor and optional exact declined decision, never a new test or authorization. Resolution appends a reason and native actor without changing original evidence; resolved recommendations cannot be carried again. Current DVI-002 access rules apply separately to every source and destination visit. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Open a second visit, compare brake measurements with the prior visit and carry a declined finding into a new inspection; date and provenance remain visible.

- **QUALITY-001 — Completion tests and return clearance.** Define pre/post-repair checklists, road-test measurements and required technician/quality sign-off; a failed or incomplete critical check blocks ready-for-return until an authorized override with reason. Physical safety clearance is always a qualified human decision. Owner/admin define job-specific versioned pre/post checklists, including explicit measurement names and units for road tests. The currently assigned native technician records immutable results and signs the exact current checklist, results and actual work; a different owner/admin supplies the independent quality decision. Owner acts for the missing manager role. Required or critical checks must pass, or an independent owner/admin explicitly selects the failed/incomplete checks and records a qualified safe-to-release override with reason; both signatures remain required. A passing retest after failure requires linked actual corrective work added after that failure, with reason. Values keep their entered units without conversion. Later work, results, checklist revision or technician change invalidates clearance. Only the current owner may take over an open repair’s technician assignment with a reason, including after fresh-owner recovery; every prior work/result/signature snapshot retains its actor, old clearance becomes stale, and the new technician needs a different current owner/admin’s independent quality decision. Archived identity attribution grants no destination authority. Agents cannot record, sign or override. Existing historical completions retain their state and never acquire invented quality evidence. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Fail the post-repair brake check, try ready status, record corrective work and obtain a second sign-off; inspect the failed result and any manager override.

- **DIAG-002 — Specialist diagnostics connections.** Attach vehicle scan-tool reports and device diagnostics from PhoneCheck and Blackbelt360 using authorized APIs or vendor result-file imports; map serial/VIN, test version and pass/fail, flag mismatches and retain raw evidence. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Import a scan report and each device provider’s test result through its adapter; a wrong serial is quarantined and no generated explanation changes measured results.

- **DIAG-003 — OEM service information and recall context.** Open licensed OEM repair procedures, fluid specifications, technical service bulletins, recall references and maintenance schedules matched to VIN/engine through MOTOR, ALLDATA and ProDemand; show provenance and licensing restrictions. Recall completion requires an authorized repair record. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Look up the same VIN in each licensed connector, attach a cited procedure/TSB and recall reference to a job, and refuse a mismatched engine or expired entitlement.

### Estimates and customer authorization

- **EST-001 — Parts and labor pricing.** Build USD estimates with quantities, labor hours/rates, part cost/selling price, fees, discounts, tax treatment and customer/fleet rate profiles; show totals before tax, tax and balance, and convert accepted lines into the repair and invoice without rekeying. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. Named customer/fleet labor-rate profiles are explicitly selected for a draft with an exact saved version; they do not create customer accounts or fleet membership. Each revision snapshots its profile and all line prices. Quantities have up to three decimals; USD unit costs and discounts are nonnegative whole cents, and a per-unit discount cannot exceed its unit selling price. Discounted unit price is extended and rounded half up to cents, then line tax is rounded half up; labor profiles replace entered labor rates before the line discount. Fees have an explicit taxable/exempt choice at the shop rate and require customer authorization and recorded actual quantity like other lines. Later profile or tax edits do not reprice accepted work or invoices. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare mixed labor/parts for a fleet rate, apply a discount and compare the accepted estimate with actuals and invoice cent totals.

- **EST-002 — Canned jobs and pricing matrices.** Version reusable service packages with parts, labor and inspection mappings; configure cost-based parts matrices, labor matrices, minimum charges and rounding, preserving the rule and override reason used on each estimate. Owner/admin version packages and part/labor cost matrices; the owner acts for the missing manager when recording a reasoned price override. Costs and minima are nonnegative USD cents per part unit or labor hour. Bands start at zero, have inclusive lower and exclusive upper bounds, meet without gaps and end unbounded. Cost plus basis-point markup rounds half-up to whole cents, then upward to the configured cent increment, then the minimum applies. Quantity is unchanged. Each saved draft freezes package, optional exact inspection-template assignment, cost, complete matrix revision, band, intermediate amounts, calculated price and any overriding actor/reason/time. Matrix or package changes affect future drafts only; no package preparation grants work or customer consent. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Create an oil-service package and cost matrix, add it from a finding, override one price as a manager, then change the matrix; the old estimate remains reproducible.

- **EST-003 — Customer approval and decline by link.** Send an expiring repair-scoped link by text/email; customers view exact version, evidence, total and terms, approve/decline individual jobs or all work and sign. Decisions retain identity, timestamp, revision, line amounts and consent evidence; a changed price invalidates the old link. Owner/admin email a link for the current person-approved, undecided revision of an estimate or EST-004 supplement through the shop’s configured mail sender to the asset’s customer email, and see it once to share it themselves; the send records the provider’s answer (handed to the provider, never “delivered”; a development outbox is labelled not sent), and text sending is MSG-001’s. Links carry 256 random bits with only the SHA-256 stored outside archives, expire after 1–30 days (default 7), are revocable and stop working once the estimate gets a new revision or total, any decision, or the repair completes. The customer page needs no session and shows the exact revision’s lines, quantities, unit prices, per-line sales tax and totals, DIAG-001 shared findings and the shop’s terms; the customer chooses approve or decline for every line, types a full name and explicitly agrees to sign and receive this record electronically, with the typed name as their electronic signature. Before submitting the decision, the page tells the customer to request a paper copy of the estimate and decision from the repair shop by phone or in person; the signed receipt retains this full consent text. One same-origin POST per link records the decision through the exact-revision authorization rules as a link-only customer actor (method “signed link”, typed name, addressee email, UTC time, revision, approved and declined line amounts and approved totals, consent text); an identical replay returns the same receipt and a different second decision is refused. The receipt is canonical JSON with its SHA-256, retrievable by owner/admin and labelled a typed-name electronic signature with identity not verified beyond possession of the emailed link. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** On a phone approve one line and decline another, replay the request and try an expired/stale link after a price change; exactly one valid decision is retained and staff can retrieve its signed receipt.

- **EST-004 — Supplemental approval on the same repair.** New findings or price changes create a separate supplementary version on the existing RO, retaining prior accepted/declined lines, completed work and decisions; only the incremental scope needs fresh consent and refusal does not stop already authorized work. Each supplement is a separately identified estimate linked to the original, with its own reason, revisions, person approval, exact manual handoff and customer decision. A prospective price change explicitly replaces an unworked quantity from an exact authorized estimate/revision/line with the same part/labor kind and quantity at a new nonnegative USD price and the current frozen shop tax basis; higher and lower prices are supported. Pending or declined replacements leave prior authority intact. Accepting the replacement reduces only the prior unworked quantity, rechecking other replacements and actuals atomically; completed quantities keep their original price and tax. Work and invoice rows bind estimate ID, revision and line ID independently, even when line IDs repeat. Every original or supplemental customer decision stores the customer authorizer, method (in person, phone, signed, email reply or other with required evidence/note), server-recorded time, native recorder and exact authorized revised total computed from that revision. Phone decisions lacking identity or evidence are refused. Every invoice snapshots each used scope’s approval and decision; saved exports preserve those sources and remain immutable. Existing ESTIMATE-001 manual sharing is labelled as a recorded handoff, never provider delivery; EST-003 and MSG-001–003 retain customer-link and delivered-messaging requirements. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Authorize brakes, start work, discover a rotor and send its supplement; decline it, then approve a new supplement and verify invoice quantities cannot cross authorized revisions.

- **EST-005 — Declined work register.** Keep each declined or deferred repair with reason, evidence, price/version, date and mileage, owner and due trigger; advisors recall it on the next visit and reprice before asking again, never count it as sold. Staff record the exact current customer decision, including a full decline with zero authorized lines, with no work authority from a decline. The register snapshots the observed reading and decision; its follow-up is due at the entered same-unit reading threshold (inclusive) or UTC date (inclusive), whichever arrives first. A later visit prepares a separate current-price draft and needs its own approval and customer decision; the prior decline stays unchanged. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Decline tires at 60,000 miles, open the next visit and turn the still-needed item into a repriced estimate with a new decision; history keeps both outcomes.

- **GUIDE-001 — MOTOR labor times.** With licensed MOTOR access, select exact vehicle/engine and operations, include overlapping-operation adjustments and source/version on an advisor-reviewed labor line; never confuse flat-rate billed time with technician clock time. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Select a MOTOR operation, add a related operation with overlap and compare imported hours/provenance to the licensed source before approval.

- **GUIDE-002 — ALLDATA labor and procedures.** Connect the shop’s ALLDATA entitlement for matched operations, labor times and linked procedures; retain source references and handle unsupported vehicles or lost entitlement as visible exceptions. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Build a quote from a licensed ALLDATA vehicle lookup, compare its operation/time, then expire access and show the unresolved lookup without fabricated times.

- **GUIDE-003 — ProDemand estimating and maintenance.** Use the shop’s Mitchell 1 ProDemand entitlement for vehicle-specific labor operations, maintenance schedules and repair information; operator reviews the selected operation and avoids duplicate included labor. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Select maintenance and repair operations from ProDemand on an RO, compare source hours and included work, and retain the reference on the approved estimate.

- **EST-006 — Estimate expiry and approval chasing.** Configure expiry and signature-request steps, send reminders only for current undecided versions, stop on reply/decision/opt-out and route unanswered or delivery-failed requests to an advisor. Owner/admin (acting as service advisor) keep one versioned chase policy: the approval-link lifetime (1–30 days, default 7) and up to three increasing reminder days (default 2 and 5), each strictly before expiry; every EST-003 link snapshots the policy when sent, and a reminder day is 24 hours after the moment the link was sent. A Worker cron every 15 minutes sends due reminders through the shop’s configured Base mail sender only while that link is the current, unrevoked, unexpired, undecided revision of an open repair; each reminder carries a fresh 256-bit token for the same link (only its SHA-256 is stored, outside archives), offers Stop reminders, and records the provider’s answer as given (handed to the provider, never “delivered”). One durable claim precedes each provider call; a run sends at most the latest due step per link and a failed or uncertain step is never retried. Reminders stop immediately on a customer decision (link or in-shop authorization) or revocation, and at the next run on a new revision or total, expiry or a closed repair; an owner/admin records a customer reply (phone, in person or email) with a note, labelled as recorded by staff because Mallet does not read mailbox replies (MSG-002/MSG-003 inherit stopping on a threaded reply before they ship). Opt-out: the customer’s approval page offers Stop reminders (same-origin POST on the link, no signature) and owner/admin may record an opt-out the customer gave; it applies to that email address installation-wide for automatic reminders only, never closes an open link or blocks an advisor’s explicit send, and is lifted only by owner/admin recording how the customer asked to resume (MSG-001 text reminders inherit the opt-out record before they ship). A rejected, rate-limited, uncertain or unconfigured send (first email or reminder) and a current revision that expires undecided (including after an opt-out) each open one advisor task on the repair and the Approvals queue, resolved by owner/admin with a note and never closed automatically; a failed first email also stops that link’s reminders. Technicians and agents cannot manage chasing, agents receive no chase records, and the scheduler and customer opt-out commands are refused at the command door. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Send an estimate, advance a scheduled reminder, supersede it and approve the replacement; only the current undecided version is chased and failed delivery opens an advisor task.

### Workshop execution and technician work

- **FLOW-001 — Live repair and technician boards.** Show intake, diagnosis, approval, parts, work, quality, ready, collected and closed stages with assignment, customer, due date, priority and blockers; filter and search, enforce allowed transitions and retain canceled/lost work. Stages derive from each repair’s own records in this precedence: closed (canceled, lost, or collected with the invoice exported), collected, ready (completed after QUALITY-001 clearance), quality (recorded work with the assigned technician’s current sign-off or a post-work check result), approval (any estimate or supplement undecided, or every one declined), parts (customer-authorized with a staff-reported required part not physically received), work (customer-authorized), diagnosis (a DIAG-001 diagnosis or DVI inspection exists), then intake; a card moves only when its gated command succeeds, so work needs customer authorization and ready needs quality clearance. Due is the current customer pickup promise, else the booked end; overdue means now is at or after the promise. Owner/admin set normal, high or urgent priority with a reason and kept history; cancel or mark lost an open repair with no recorded work and no running timer (reason, stage and actor kept, bay and technician freed, shown under Closed); and record collection only on a completed repair whose invoice a person approved: staff type the collector’s name, and the immutable record keeps the recorder, time and invoice number and status, labelled “Collection recorded by staff. Mallet did not record or verify payment, keys or the collector’s identity.” RETURN-001 adds its settlement gate, unpaid-release override with reason, delegate authority, keys/accessories, mileage-out, receipt and warranty advice to that record before it ships. Filters cover one technician (lead or helper), stage, priority, customer-promise status and search over customer, email, vehicle, plate/VIN, title and invoice number; technicians see only repairs they are on; the board re-reads current records on every load and every 60 seconds. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Move several realistic ROs through the board, filter one technician and overdue customer promise, and try to skip authorization/quality gates.

- **FLOW-002 — Promise times and capacity.** Set a customer promise separately from bay booking; show parts ETA, labor remaining and resource dependencies, warn before a promise is missed, and require an advisor to approve and communicate any revised promise. Owner/admin act as the service advisor and approve original promises or exact proposed revisions; assigned native technicians may update required-part ETAs and resource dependencies only for currently assigned repairs. The pickup promise is separate from the bay/technician booking. Every part or dependency edit appends its source, reason, actor and UTC time; a staff-reported ETA is never supplier confirmation or physical receipt. Labor remaining is current customer-authorized sold labor less performed quantities and accepted replacements, excluding timers. The planning finish is the latest of now, booking start, unreceived part ETAs, and unfinished dependency booking/projected finishes, plus remaining labor hours. The plan warns before pickup when that finish exceeds the promise or an unreceived part is past ETA; now at or beyond pickup is overdue. These calculations never move a booking. Revising a promise requires an owner/admin to review the exact prior promise, plan, dependency versions, recipient and remaining work, then approve and claim one email through the configured Base mail sender. The original promise and all revisions remain. Receipts retain exact old/new times, reason, approving actor, recipient and provider outcome; accepted means handed to the provider, development means not sent, and failed or unconfirmed notices remain visible for advisor action without automatic retries. Customer pickup promises and mail render in the configured installation time zone with its IANA name (America/New_York by default), preserving daylight-saving changes. Owner/admin may change the shop zone with a reason; its version is rechecked before approving a notice, and each notice retains the zone used. Staff operational times may use the browser zone while showing the shop zone. Historical claimed sends cannot be replayed after restore. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Delay a required part beyond a promised pickup, inspect the affected bay/technician plan and approve one revised promise; preserve original promise and customer notice receipt.

- **FLOW-003 — Rules-based routing and escalation.** Route intake by location, repair type, skill and priority; escalate unassigned, approval-waiting, parts-stalled or diagnostic exceptions to a named manager on shop-hour deadlines, with acknowledgment and resolution history. Each blocker episode snapshots its policy and calendar; deadlines count whole real minutes whose shop-local starts are open (opening inclusive, closing exclusive), skipping closed dates, counting repeated DST minutes twice and missing minutes zero. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit two repair types, run routing and advance a closed-day deadline; one assigned handler receives each case and an unacknowledged blocker escalates once.

- **TIME-001 — Employee clock and job timers.** Technicians clock in/out, break and start/pause/resume job labor from their assigned board; distinguish attendance, actual time, sold hours and nonbillable work, require reasons for corrections and handle concurrent timers by shop policy. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Hand a diagnostic job to a specialist, mention an advisor, complete a dependent subtask and inspect both technicians’ time and handover receipt.

It identifies itself, preserves transcript and escalates ambiguity, safety, disputes, discounts and new scope. Customer replies never become consent unless captured through EST-003. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve an exact estimate message, ask a factual question and then demand a discount/safety assurance; send only authorized facts, escalate the exceptions and keep line decisions unchanged until the customer signs.

## Honest boundaries and connected authorities

Mallet owns the complete repair-shop job above. It does not act as a bank, card network, lender, parcel carrier, OEM service franchise, physical diagnostic instrument, tax advisor or full general ledger. The shop uses its merchant-owned Stripe/Square accounts and financing partners, ShipStation/carriers, licensed labor/OEM programs, technician-confirmed tests and QuickBooks Online/Xero or Pantry. Integrating each required account, approval program and data agreement is part of delivery. Provider refusal or missing eligibility is visible and leaves the affected job pending; it never fabricates a result or removes the contractual integration.

The first buyer is US independent auto repair. Tire, fleet, bicycle, device, mail-in and equipment workflows remain expressly included at the named clauses’ depth. Manufacturing, general warehouse planning, payroll/tax filing ([Envelope](https://github.com/just-every/run-envelope)) and broad marketing campaign management stay in their separate products; Mallet exports staff time, finance and consented repair events to those authorities. Shop repair reminders, declined-work recovery and review follow-up are native Mallet workflows. Physical diagnosis, road-test and safe return are qualified technician decisions; consumer financing is underwritten and disclosed by the provider.
