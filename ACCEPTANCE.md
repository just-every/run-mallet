# Mallet target acceptance

Acceptance journeys for the target edition of 17 October 2026, judged against code and tests at `88562eb529abd66394171d97778854c4a94fa4b2`. Each paragraph retains the existing journey form and adds its clause ID and current status. `done` means the cited implementation and exercising tests support that clause; it is not production-provider or hosted acceptance. Partial/missing journeys are build work and must pass through public doors with realistic shop data. Only paid/network edges may be faked in tests.

Run the complete US repair: vehicle identification and inspection → priced estimate and line/supplement consent → approved supplier order and receiving → assigned technician work/quality → USD invoice and settled payment → documented return → mileage/declined-work follow-up and reconciled reports. Repeat appropriate bench journeys for bicycle/device assets. Use 1440 and 390 widths for staff and customer screens and real iOS/Android installs for native claims. Import source history through the named move-in door; never substitute seeded application records.

**DVI-002 — Photo and video inspection evidence.** Status: **done**. Capture marked-up brake photos and a short video, interrupt the upload, resume, and view original/shared media from the finding; duplicates and private originals are controlled. Evidence: `src/mallet/media/http.ts`; `src/mallet/media/service.ts`; `src/mallet/ui/media.ts`; `migrations/core/0019_inspection_media.sql`; `tests/media.test.mjs`; `tests/media-browser.mjs` (DVI-002). Owner/admin sessions may access all shop findings; technicians access only their currently assigned repairs, and agents have no media access. Every upload, resume and download rechecks native authority and assignment; writes are same-origin. Findings and uploads bind opaque client IDs and exact metadata, 64 KiB chunk ordinals and SHA-256; mismatched retries refuse, and completion verifies the whole digest before viewing. JPEG/PNG/WebP originals are at most 8 MiB and MP4/WebM originals at most 32 MiB. The phone camera/file door retains original bytes; the annotated shared photo is a JPEG at quality 0.78 with its longest edge at most 1600 pixels. Browser video capture records up to 30 seconds at requested video/audio bitrates of 1,500,000/64,000 bits per second; a prepared shared video retains the captured compressed bytes. Capture time is technician-reported with a timezone and normalized to UTC, separate from server receipt time and native actor provenance. Both versions stay private, with no public URLs; “shared” means prepared, not sent. The browser remembers only opaque finding/upload IDs and content hashes for reselect-and-resume. DVI-003 owns reviewed customer delivery and must preserve private-original control; GOV-006 owns retention, erasure and holds, with no automatic media deletion before that policy ships. New product data inherits OPS-001 before shipping.
