# Customer retention, erasure and holds

The owner opens **Data & access → Open retention & erasure**. Select a linked customer account, download a private data package, place a scoped hold, or preview an erasure. The package includes exact shared import originals that can contain other customers; review it before giving a copy to anyone.

The default retains indefinitely. A policy sets media and mutable personal-profile lifetimes in days, but never schedules deletion. Preview **Run approved age-based retention**, then explicitly approve the exact candidates. Active repairs and unknown completion dates do not age out. Completed and invoiced repairs can qualify. Release a hold with a reason before reviewing those candidates again.

Every deletion needs a fresh owner confirmation, a current preview, a reason, consent evidence and the typed confirmation. Holds and concurrent record/media changes invalidate that preview. A financial hold preserves financial history while eligible media can be erased; media, personal, import and all-data holds protect their scope. Shared import artifacts are withdrawn whole. Until there is a precise cross-customer artifact map, any active import/all hold protects shared import custody.

Customer erasure destroys temporary unlock keys in R2 before processing profiles or media. Restoring D1 ciphertext cannot reconstruct those destroyed keys. Current photos, videos, originals, shared variants, finding metadata, incomplete upload chunks and their aliases are in D1 and are deleted there. Historical financial, authorization, signed intake/custody and merge-consent snapshots, asset identity/service history and contacts shared with other accounts remain preserved. The preview and receipt explain these boundaries.

The receipt records actor, consent evidence, counts, held scope and partial progress. A failure requires a fresh review; no destructive action retries in the background. Export a fresh workshop backup after erasure and retain it as the later journal for older backups. A known later epoch or supplied later journal refuses an older archive. A fresh destination without later source evidence cannot establish freshness and labels that history unverified.

GOV-006 remains partial pending ct approval of these conservative retention and eligibility defaults.
