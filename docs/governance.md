# Staff delegation and private audit

Open Data & access, then Staff roles and limits or Search and export audit history. Only the current owner can configure delegation or inspect audit history. Granting and revoking rights needs a fresh sign-in confirmation or the owner’s enrolled second factor. An existing administrator cannot grant these roles.

The owner grants an active native member one role: advisor, manager, purchaser, cashier or accountant. The member opens Staff workspace after sign-in. New grants start with zero limits. Discount settings use percent on the form and integer basis points at the API. Spend and refund forms use USD; the API stores whole cents. Grants apply only to this installation’s shop. Other named locations and every unlisted transport are refused. Existing native technician assignments and legacy owner and administrator authority remain separate.

Advisors prepare and approve estimates, within explicit per-unit discount limits. A revision keeps the original line identity and cannot compound price reductions across revisions. Managers may perform the same work, manage native stock and purchase orders, and record bounded native finance actions. Purchasers manage stock and purchase orders under their spend limit. Purchase commands require a separate cost-view right; that right never grants a new command. Cashiers record tender and bounded native credit and manual refund observations after fresh confirmation. Accountants have read and download access to the permitted native finance records. The accountant workspace does not implement reconciliation. No delegated role can submit a provider refund, redirect a failed refund, submit a supplier order, change settings, mint credentials, erase records or grant roles.

The grant and native session are checked again inside each write transaction. Revoking a grant, changing its revision or deactivating the member ends the old authority. Grant records are excluded from backups; the audit snapshots remain historical evidence after a fresh-owner restore and cannot grant destination access.

The private audit screen searches structured receipts by text, exact action, actor ID and inclusive UTC dates. Each page has at most 100 entries; use Next audit page to continue. JSON and CSV downloads contain the selected page and retain the selected filters. CSV cells neutralize spreadsheet formulas. Entries contain actor, timestamp, policy, before and after revision references and evidence identifiers. Source estimates, approvals, stock corrections and credit records retain their exact decisions. Command bodies, bearer tokens, sealed credentials and raw provider bodies are not copied into the audit. Corrections create additional receipts. Native import batches bind the actual person in the same database transaction, independently of the portable owner-role principal.
