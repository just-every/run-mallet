# Repair profitability

The owner opens **Repair orders → Repair profitability**. The report and its source JSON use `/app/reports/profitability` and `/api/mallet/reports/profitability`. Loaded labor settings live at `/app/settings/profitability`; `costRate.set` is the equivalent native command. Admins, technicians, delegated staff, customer sessions and agents receive no profitability or loaded-rate access. Loaded rates are omitted from non-owner workshop state.

The CT ruling dated 26 October 2026 approves the personal-owner-only slice with riders. REPORT-002 remains PARTIAL. The existing payroll report and payroll wage records keep their meaning. This report never sends, pays, posts to accounting or destroys data.

## Basis

The inclusive UTC date range selects repairs by their booking creation date. Current later invoices, credits, corrections and warranty cases remain attached to that cohort. It is a current repair-cohort view, not a filing-period or settlement report. All amounts are USD cents; gross profit is pretax revenue less cost, and margin is gross profit divided by positive revenue, half-up to basis points. No positive revenue means no margin percentage.

Estimated revenue uses the exact customer-authorized lines after authorized supplement replacements, already net of per-unit discounts. Explicit line cost snapshots supply unit part cost and hourly estimated labor cost; a linked sublet's quoted scope cost is already a total. An ordinary fee has no direct cost unless an explicit cost or sublet supplies one. Missing estimated cost is unavailable, never silently treated as a free part or free labor.

Realized revenue requires an approved or exported invoice. Issued credit-note pretax amounts reverse revenue once; completing its refund does not reverse it again. Draft invoices and open repairs cannot establish realized gross profit. Costs can still be inspected while a repair is open.

## Source costs

- Consumed part cost is the retained `stockUse.costCents`, linked to the received lot and its original unit cost. Receiving or reserving unused inventory is not another repair expense. Inspected saleable returns reverse the cumulative proportion of that exact consumed cost, with the final return preserving the last cent.
- For purchase-linked lots, freight and supplier tax come from the latest matched native supplier bill, covering every receipt and the current short-close decision. Missing or exception bills leave realized cost unavailable. Charges allocate cumulatively by received pretax line value, then receipt quantity, original lot quantity and net consumed quantity. Zero-value bills use received line quantities. Untraced and unused inventory retains its share. Transfer descendants retain the original lot family rather than receiving another charge. Saleable returns reduce the current allocated charges by their net consumed quantities. Each event exposes its bill identity, revision, supplier invoice reference, receipt, original lot, stock use, source cents, allocated cents and basis. Standalone receipts have no separately retained tax or freight fields; the report cannot reconstruct charges that were never recorded.
- Reconciled supplier core and installed-part RMA credits reduce cost once. Expected credits are never counted. Customer core credits are ordinary issued credit notes, separate from supplier cost recovery.
- Sublets use only the latest bill covering every retained scope. A bill spanning multiple scopes is allocated cumulatively by scope quantity, retaining its bill revision and source amounts. Customer sublet charges remain invoice fee lines and are never added twice.
- Job timers use their latest approved revision, including billable, nonbillable and rework categories. Each interval uses the owner's declared loaded hourly cost effective on its UTC start date, half-up to cents. Running intervals and missing rates make realized cost incomplete. Attendance and idle wages are not invented as repair hours. Job labor cost is allocated cumulatively across recorded labor quantities; unallocated job time remains explicit.
- Loaded cost is an owner-declared full hourly amount with a required basis and reason, including the owner's chosen wage and burden components. Dated revisions remain immutable; greatest effective date wins, latest save on ties. Future dates never apply early. A dated correction can recompute historical costs while retaining every earlier rate revision. Source JSON exposes `rateHistory`: the full preceding and new declarations, their effective UTC dates, USD-cent amounts, basis and reason, actor and timestamps, joined to the native audit entry and its before and after revision references. The first declaration has no preceding value. Legacy declarations without retained audit references expose a null audit, never invented provenance.
- Explicit warranty actual costs replace the stock or allocated timer cost for those same actual quantities. Only a credited warranty claim outcome reduces cost. If an RMA and warranty claim could duplicate recovery, the cost is unavailable until reconciled; no matching or duplicate reduction is guessed.

Every cost event retains its source identity, amount and relevant received cost, bill revision, rate, timer history or warranty evidence. The report links each contributing repair and its sources. Reads create no product records.

Original repair adjusted profit includes the linked comeback's net cost, including its own revenue and recorded recovery. Cohort totals count each repair once; the adjusted original is a comparison, not another expense in totals.

Comparisons cover repair, line category, technician, advisor, service and supplier. Advisor means the original booking recorder. Non-timer attribution uses the current lead; actual timer costs use the timer owner. Service grouping uses the immutable priced package name and revision retained on the authorized estimate when present. Lines and corresponding cost events retain that package identity and complete snapshot; later catalog edits do not relabel history. Lines without package provenance use their exact retained description, with a null package source. Unallocated job time and warranty recovery remain explicit.

Mixed-supplier part sales and estimated costs allocate cumulatively by linked consumed quantities; realized sales use quantities net of saleable returns. Untraced authorized or invoiced quantities remain unattributed. Source lines expose each supplier, its separate estimated and realized quantity weights and linked stock uses. Discounts and issued credits have already reduced the revenue being allocated. Sublet and core sales use their uniquely linked supplier. Every comparison reconciles to cohort revenue and cost without inventing an association.

## Remaining scope and recovery

Multiple locations require SHOP-001 and are not available. Finer cost permissions remain GOV-001. Native matched purchase bills and retained package snapshots now support the bounded allocations above. Complete historical package attribution remains impossible where source estimates lack a retained priced package snapshot; descriptions cannot establish a missing package association. Standalone receipts lacking retained separate supplier charges cannot establish those charges. These missing-source cases and the remaining comparison and access scope keep REPORT-002 PARTIAL.

The existing registered profitability journey additionally exercises split receipts and unused inventory, a mixed-supplier package, missing and exception bills, freight and supplier-tax penny allocation, fractional saleable returns and customer credits, immutable package attribution after a catalog revision, complete rate declarations and native audit linkage, earlier and later effective-date selection, same-date revision precedence and fresh-owner archive recovery. The browser fixture keeps its established totals. This bounded proof does not complete the clause or supersede the exact CT ruling in CONTRACT and ACCEPTANCE.

OPS-001 archives retain loaded cost revisions, original cost sources, credits, timer corrections and warranty evidence. A fresh native owner recomputes the same report; archived actors and original sessions confer no destination authority. Invalid loaded-rate records are refused during private archive staging.
