# Private conversation drafts

The current owner opens **Private conversation drafts** from a repair. This is a private preparation and review workspace. It does not send messages or ingest authenticated replies, and a review does not grant sending authority.

Enter the exact question, its stated source, the statement time with a timezone, a reference to the supporting evidence and a unique capture reference. Telephone notes, in-person statements, entered email text and entered transcripts are staff records. None establishes an authenticated customer-channel receipt or consent.

Two native factual templates are available:

- **Inspection findings:** “What did the inspection find?” uses the explicitly shared, reviewed inspection report.
- **Approved estimate prices:** “What is the approved estimate price?” uses the exact current person-approved estimate revision already shared with the customer.

The report must name this repair, cite current inspection results and the same estimate revision, and have a recorded accepted handoff to the current customer's address through an unrevoked, unexpired report link. A provider handoff does not establish customer receipt. Private technician notes, supplier costs and original media are not copied into drafts. Report explanations are the already reviewed customer-facing explanations.

Missing or changed sources, unsupported questions, ambiguity, safety assurances, disputes, discounts and new scope require human escalation. The owner can approve only the exact current factual draft text and revision. Changed sources invalidate the earlier review; preparing a fresh revision retains the earlier draft, citations and attributable review history. The two supported questions are deliberately narrow; other wording goes to a person.

Drafts, entered replies and reviews cannot change estimate lines, signed decisions, repair authority, promises or prices. Those actions retain their existing separate approval doors. Preparation keys retain their original four commands.

Eligible history is included in owner customer exports and installation archives. A fresh destination owner can inspect that history; no conversation credential, sending capability or automated action is restored. Private question text, source evidence, capture references, drafts and review notes follow eligible personal-data erasure and existing scoped holds. The existing portability erasure journal rejects an older archive when supplied the later trusted journal. No new retention duration or automatic destruction is introduced.

ADV-002 remains partial. Approved customer-facing shop and channel reply policy, actual sending, authenticated reply ingestion, autonomous conversation and the complete signed-customer conversation journey remain unbuilt or unproved. Scoped delegation and provider boundaries remain outside this private slice.
