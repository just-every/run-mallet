# Additional work on the same repair

Keep the original estimate and its customer decision. On the repair order, open **Prepare an incremental supplement**, explain the new finding and prepare only the additional lines. Approve that exact revision, print/share it, record the actual handoff and record the customer's line choices. The handoff records what staff did; it does not send email or text or claim delivery. A full decline leaves earlier authorized work available.

For an unworked price change, choose **Change the price of unworked authorized scope**, select the earlier line and enter the quantity and new USD unit price. The customer separately decides this replacement. Acceptance transfers only that unworked quantity to the new price and current shop tax basis. Declining leaves the earlier price authorized. Completed quantities keep their original price and tax. A conflicting intervening work record or accepted replacement refuses the decision and requires a new reviewed proposal.

The command API uses `estimate.prepare` with the current `jobId` and `version`, `supplement: true`, a required `reason`, and incremental `lines`. A price-change line also supplies `replaces: {estimateId, revision, lineId}`; its own positive quantity is the exact quantity to replace, and it must retain the source's part/labor kind. Both higher and lower nonnegative unit prices are supported. Before a decision, `estimate.revise` creates an unapproved revision. After a decision, retain that record and prepare another supplement.

Approval, recorded sharing and customer authorization use the existing estimate commands with the exact supplement ID, record version and revision. Agents retain their existing four preparation commands and cannot decide, share or authorize. Assigned technicians use `work.record` with the current job version and the exact `estimateId`, `revision`, `lineId` and quantity. Original-estimate callers may omit the estimate ID/revision for compatibility; supplements require the exact revision. Line IDs may repeat across scopes and never share a quantity allowance.

After the current quality signatures and completion, invoice preparation combines actual quantities at their individual authorized prices and tax. Each invoice source snapshots its person approval and customer decision; each line records its source estimate and revision. The saved CSV includes `EstimateId` and each line's `EstimateRevision`. Prior saved exports remain unchanged and repeated export retrieves the same saved entry. Native archives include all supplements, original decisions, actuals, source snapshots and saved bytes, with fresh identity and authority on restore.
