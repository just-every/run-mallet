# Repair status email and pickup follow-up

Open a repair and choose **Status & pickup notices**. Record the customer’s affirmative email permission and its evidence. The current owner then reviews a customer-safe message for the current diagnosis, approval-needed, parts-delay, ready or pickup stage. Internal diagnostic evidence is never copied into the message.

Each approval names one exact template revision and source event. Set the recipient timezone, allowed hours, send instant and expiry. A changed repair, customer contact, permission or stage suppresses the pending message. A cron pass checks current eligibility before one Base mail handoff. Provider acceptance means handoff, not delivery. The exact content, actor, claim time, outcome and provider reference remain inspectable on this screen. Failed or interrupted handoffs never retry automatically.

For ready and pickup notices, record the shop’s follow-up deadline and policy. Failed contact and uncollected repairs appear in **Waiting for you** on the repair list. Open the task, contact the customer personally and record the resolution. Collection suppresses uncollected-property escalation. No fee, disposal or title action occurs automatically.

Record withdrawal on the same screen when a customer asks to stop. Pending and future repair-status email is suppressed. Owner approval cannot override withdrawal. SMS and provider delivery acknowledgment are not implemented by this email path.

Permission, approvals, outcomes and tasks survive a private archive. Local send authority is excluded: restored approvals send nothing and create no new escalation task. Fresh destination permission and a new eligible event require fresh owner approval. Personal erasure revokes local authority, including under a hold; exact permission and approval history remains with the existing authorization custody. No new retention schedule is introduced.
