# Issued invoice copies and tax custody

The owner opens an approved repair invoice with **Issue, print and email invoice copies**. Review the recorded USD amounts, readings, tender allocations and warranty wording, then issue a permanent numbered copy. Download the same PDF or text later, or print the HTML copy. A corrected copy requires the latest copy and a reason; the earlier copy remains readable. These corrections do not alter financial amounts. Use the existing reviewed credit-note workflow for financial reversals.

Email is an explicit owner action to the invoice customer or the current recorded payer. Record the customer’s email request and confirm the exact recipient and copy. One copy gets one attempt. Provider acceptance means handed to the provider, not confirmed customer delivery. An uncertain or interrupted attempt stays unconfirmed and never retries automatically. A restored copy cannot reuse its former owner’s sending authority; the new owner must issue a reviewed corrected copy and obtain fresh email consent. Text delivery is not implemented.

The PDF uses a standard Latin font. Characters outside ASCII have substitute visible glyphs, while PDF ActualText, HTML and text copies retain the exact Unicode content. Use HTML or text when visible non-ASCII names are required. Issued copies and delivery outcomes travel in the owner archive, without sessions or credentials.

On **Shop tax**, the owner can open certificate custody and the invoice tax report. Record the exact supplied certificate text, account, declared scope, jurisdiction, start date, inclusive expiry date and review evidence. Dates are UTC calendar dates; expired and future certificates are labelled. This custody does not verify eligibility or apply an exemption. Certificates remain private owner records, with no automatic deletion or outbound action.

The owner may assign one explicit jurisdiction declaration to an approved invoice. The report keeps original invoice tax less separately issued credit-note tax reversals and links each total to its repair. It also shows cumulative tax allocated to staff-recorded cash and processor-confirmed captured Stripe receipts. Partial collections allocate the original invoice tax in proportion to the original gross total, rounded half up once per invoice. Only a completed original-tender refund reverses its exact credit-note tax, bounded by tax collected. A pending, failed or uncertain refund does not reverse collected tax. Reading the report changes no record and sends nothing.

Checks, other tender and customer-credit allocations are excluded from collected tax because clearance or original cash basis is unproved. Account-credit transfers and redirected refunds carry an explicit review warning. Missing jurisdictions remain visibly unassigned. These are cumulative operational totals: complete filing-period totals and unresolved tender and credit attribution remain unfinished. Staff cash is declared by the shop, not independently verified. This report is not a completed tax return or legal advice.

Certificate application creates a new draft only for the certificate's unchanged billing account, within its inclusive UTC dates and declared scope. The owner records the legal basis and explicit customer consent for shop fee and discount rules. Discounts round each remaining unit price half up to USD cents before quantity extension, tax and percentage fees; USD 0.005 rounds up to USD 0.01. Percentage fees use discounted pre-tax parts or labor, half-up cents and the saved cap. A fixed fee above its saved cap is refused, including an older rule accepted by a previous version. Earlier revisions and invoices stay unchanged. The new draft still needs renewed approval and customer authorization. Mallet does not verify legal permission and takes no outbound action when applying these rules.
